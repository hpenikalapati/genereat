GenerEat Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In short: GenerEat does not collect, store or sell any personal information. Your recipes stay on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Information we collect

None. GenerEat has no servers and no user accounts, and the developer never receives your recipes, folders, tags, notes or any other information about you or how you use the app.

Where your recipes are stored

Everything you add to GenerEat is saved only on your device. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include GenerEat's data, as it does for other apps; that is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Importing recipes from websites

When you paste a link and tap Import, GenerEat downloads that web page directly from the website to read the recipe. Recipe photos are also loaded from the websites they come from. Like any visit from a web browser, those websites can see your device's IP address and the request, under their own privacy policies. GenerEat sends them nothing else and only contacts a website when you ask it to, or to show a photo from a recipe you saved.

Sharing recipes

When you share a recipe or folder, GenerEat creates a file or text on your device and hands it to the app you choose (such as Messages, Mail or AirDrop). It goes only to the people you send it to, through that app. GenerEat does not keep a copy anywhere else.

Tracking, ads and third parties

GenerEat contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking code, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

GenerEat does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
