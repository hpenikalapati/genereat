Everything a recipe box should do

🌐

Save from the web

Paste a link from your favorite recipe site and GenerEat pulls in the ingredients, steps, times and photo. No ads, no life story.

✍️

Write your own

Keep family recipes and your own creations alongside the ones you find online.

📁

Organize your way

Make folders for anything: holidays, meal prep, Grandma's cooking. Add tags like quick or vegetarian to find things fast.

🎲

Surprise Me

Can't decide? Pick a few tags and GenerEat suggests a recipe that fits. Don't like it? Tap again.

💌

Share recipes

Send a recipe or a whole folder to a friend. If they have GenerEat, it opens right in the app.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your recipes stay on your device.

Made for the kitchen counter

On iPad, recipes spread out with the ingredients beside the steps, so you can prop it up and cook without scrolling.
