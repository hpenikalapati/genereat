GenerEat Support

Help with saving, organizing and sharing your recipes.

Need help or found a bug? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com. Tell us which iPhone or iPad you have, your iOS version, and what happened. If a recipe link wouldn't import, include the link.

Adding recipes

How do I write my own recipe?

In the Recipes tab, tap + and choose Write a Recipe. Put one ingredient or step on each line. To split the steps into parts, add a line like == Sauce ==.

How do I save a recipe from a website?

Copy the recipe page's link, then in GenerEat tap + › Import from the Web and paste it. GenerEat fills in the title, ingredients, steps, times and photo. You can change anything before tapping Save.

Why didn't a recipe import?
  • The site blocks automatic downloads. Some websites refuse apps that read their pages. Copy the recipe in by hand with Write a Recipe.
  • Only the title came through. The page doesn't include standard recipe data, so GenerEat could only read its title. Add the rest yourself.
  • No connection. Importing needs an internet connection. Your saved recipes don't.

Organizing

What's the difference between folders and tags?

Folders are collections you name yourself, like “Holidays” or “Mom's Recipes”. A recipe can be in more than one. Tags are short words like quick, vegetarian or chicken that describe a recipe. Surprise Me uses tags to pick recipes.

How do I rename or delete a folder?

Swipe left on the folder, or touch and hold it. Deleting a folder doesn't delete the recipes in it.

Surprise Me

How does Surprise Me choose a recipe?

Pick some tags and tap Suggest a Recipe. With Any of these, a recipe needs at least one of your tags. Recipes with more of them are more likely to be picked. With All of these, a recipe needs every tag. Pick no tags to choose from everything. Tapping Suggest Another won't repeat the recipe you just saw.

It says no recipes match.

Try fewer tags, switch to “Any of these”, or set From back to All recipes.

Sharing

How do I send a recipe to a friend?

Open the recipe and tap Share:

  • Send to a GenerEat User sends a file your friend can open straight into their GenerEat app, with ingredients, steps, tags and photo link.
  • Share as Text sends a plain-text copy anyone can read.

To send a whole folder, open it and choose Share Folder from the ⋯ menu.

Someone sent me a recipe file. How do I open it?

Tap the file in Messages, Mail or AirDrop and choose GenerEat. You can review the recipe before saving it. With a shared folder, you can add every recipe at once into a folder with the same name.

Your data

Do my recipes sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Not yet. Recipes are saved only on the device where you added them. To move them to another device, share a folder to yourself with AirDrop and open it there.

Will I lose my recipes if I get a new phone?

Not if you restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, because the backup includes GenerEat's data. Deleting the app deletes its recipes, so share your folders to yourself first if you're unsure.

Does GenerEat collect my information?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy for details.
